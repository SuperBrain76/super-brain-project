## 1. What SuperBrain is (one-liners)

- **Elevator:** SuperBrain is a free sports-prediction game. Call the scores, climb the table, and beat your mates — across Europe's biggest football leagues and now ice hockey.
- **Tagline options:** "Predict. Compete. Grow your SuperBrain." · "Think you know football? Prove it." · "One tap a match. Free."
- **Category:** Free-to-play sports predictor / social prediction game. **Not gambling** — no stake, no cash out, skill-based bragging rights and an in-app IQ score.
- **Where:** superbrain.social (web, works on phone). Native iOS/Android app in the pipeline.

## 2. How it works (accurate mechanics — never misstate these)

- **Predict scores, one tap.** Pick Home / Draw / Away for a fast pick, or set an exact scoreline to chase max points.
- **Scoring:** exact score = 5, right goal difference = 3, right result = 2, wrong = 0.
- **The Banker:** nominate one match a week to *double* its points. Risk/reward.
- **Streaks:** predict every week to build a streak.
- **IQ:** your in-app currency/score — earn it by being right. "Grow your SuperBrain."
- **Private leagues:** create a league, invite friends, settle who actually knows ball.
- **League tables + form guide**, real fixtures, and **kickoff times auto-converted to the viewer's local timezone** (Dubai, Stockholm, London — no mental math).

## 3. What's live (the product to sell today)

- **Football — 5 leagues, 2026/27:** Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1. ~1,750 real matches.
- **Ice Hockey — SHL (Sweden), 2026/27:** 14 clubs, 364 games. Season opens **19 Sep 2026**.
- **Coming (tease, don't over-promise dates):** UEFA Champions League and the NHL — activate the moment their real schedules drop.
- **Total:** 6 leagues, 2 sports, 110+ clubs, 2,100+ matches. **Free, always.**

## 4. Why it's different (the hooks)

1. **Free & skill, not gambling.** No money in, no money out — just who's smartest. Safe to share anywhere.
2. **Beat your mates.** Private leagues are the point. It's a group-chat sport.
3. **Real everything.** Real fixtures, real results, real tables — not made-up scenarios.
4. **One tap to play, two to compete.** Zero friction to start; depth (exact scores, banker) when you want it.
5. **Now beyond football.** Ice hockey (SHL) is live — Swedish market wedge + a genuinely under-served predictor niche.
6. **Global-friendly.** Kickoff times in *your* timezone automatically.

## 6. Brand voice for marketing

- **Energetic, broadcast-style, banter-forward** — matchday hype, hot takes, playful trash talk. This is the *marketing* voice (louder than the calm in-product tone).
- **Confident, never scammy.** No fake urgency, no "guaranteed wins," no odds/gambling language. We sell *bragging rights and fun*, not money.
- **Fan-first.** Speak the culture (nicknames, rivalries, "who ya got?"), stay neutral between clubs.
- **Inclusive & global.** Works for a fan in Dubai, Stockholm, or London.
- Signature line to reinforce: **"Grow your SuperBrain."**

## 7. Content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Matchweek hype** — "Gameweek is here. Who ya got?" fixture graphics, poll-style CTAs.
2. **Prediction takes / previews** — bold calls on the weekend's biggest match; invite the crowd to disagree.
3. **Results & leaderboard drama** — "The table doesn't lie." Who nailed it, upsets, banker wins/fails.
4. **Beat-your-mates** — private-league angle, group-chat memes, "tag the mate who always loses."
5. **Mechanics explainers** — banker, streaks, exact scores, IQ — short how-it-works.
6. **Topical / rivalry moments** — derbies, title races, SHL storylines.
7. **Social proof / milestones** — "X predictions this weekend", biggest IQ hauls.

## 12. Guardrails (hard rules for generated content)

- **Never** frame as betting/gambling; no odds, stakes, "winnings," or "guaranteed."
- **Stay club-neutral** — hype the game, not one team (unless clearly playful).
- **No fabricated stats, fixtures, or results** — only real, current data.
- **Accurate mechanics** — scoring is 5/3/2/0, banker doubles, it's free.
- Responsible/inclusive tone; nothing that mocks individuals or groups.
- Every asset ends with a link + a share/invite CTA.

## 13. Cadence (tied to the fixture calendar)

- **Rhythm follows matchweeks**, not a flat daily slot: preview (pre-weekend) → hype (matchday) → results/leaderboard (post).
- Football: weekly cycle (+ midweek European nights later). SHL: gamedays Tue/Thu/Sat from 19 Sep.
- Higher volume on X; 3–5 IG/TikTok assets per matchweek; email reminder each matchweek.

## 14. Launch moment

- **Timing is ideal:** the football season is underway now and **SHL opens 19 Sep** — two launch beats. Lead with football, use SHL as the "we're more than football" surprise and the Sweden wedge.
- Suggested first campaign: **"Predict the weekend — free"** across the 5 leagues, with a "start a private league with your group chat" push.
