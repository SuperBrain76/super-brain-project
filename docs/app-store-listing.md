## Description (max 4000 chars)

Predict every match. Beat your friends. Become Champion.

SuperBrain is the ultimate FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction game — free to play, open to everyone, and with a real prize for the winner.

PREDICT ALL 104 MATCHES
Submit your score predictions before every match kicks off. The earlier you predict, the better. Predictions lock automatically at kick-off — no last-minute cheating.

SCORING SYSTEM
• Exact score correct → 5 points
• Correct result (win/draw/loss) → 3 points
• Every point counts across all 104 matches

PRIVATE LEAGUES
Create a league and invite your friends, colleagues, or family. Share a code and compete on your own private leaderboard. See exactly how your predictions compared to theirs after every match.

GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
Compete against everyone playing worldwide. Climb the global rankings as the tournament progresses. Updated in real-time after every match.

WIN THE SUPERBRAIN CHAMPION WATCH
The player with the most points at the end of the FIFA World Cup 2026 wins a luxury timepiece — hand-assembled in Sweden with a Swiss mechanical movement and personalised winner engraving. The ultimate prize for the ultimate predictor.

LIVE SCORES & RESULTS
Follow every match in-app. Live scores update automatically. See how your predictions scored the moment the final whistle blows.

MATCH DAY REMINDERS
Opt in to email notifications and never miss a prediction. Get a reminder before the first match of each day, and a standings update after the last game.

FREE TO PLAY
No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. Just football and bragging rights.
